# Persona rewrites arrive only as labelled, inactive variants

The master spec says help at sentence level or above arrives as a question, a pattern from the writer's past, or an example, never as drop-in text. At tp53's explicit request (2026-11-10), Phase 1's editor personas (New Yorker, NYT, Vanity Fair editors, a favourite writer, and "you, from your own history") may propose rewrites of words, lines, paragraphs, openings and closes. They arrive only as extra variants under the writer's text: labelled with the persona, never active until the writer flips to one, and marked as machine-written in the authorship ledger. Critiques in a persona's voice arrive as margin notes. The piece never changes without the writer's flip, which keeps the thesis's core (the writer chooses and owns every line) while giving them the sparring they asked for. The blind voice test and ownership score are the tripwires if it homogenises their voice.
