# write-on

A reproduction of Jason Fried's Write_On (alternatives at word, sentence and paragraph level, Ghost, Overflow) as the base for a writing thought partner that learns the writer's style and nudges, never writes for them.

- This repo is public. Never commit Jason Fried's demo video or its frames; they live in a private repo.
- Read `GLOSSARY.md` and `docs/adr/` before changing the editor; use the glossary's terms in code, tests and issues.
- Decisions and their reasons are logged in `docs/decisions/`.
